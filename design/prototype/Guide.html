Reading the rate

01

What it is

One gram of 22K gold in Tamil Nadu today, before making charges and GST.

02

How it's worked out

The world gold price in rupees, plus 15% import duty and a small shop premium.

03

How to read it

Shops across Tamil Nadu quote close to this. A sovereign is 8 grams.

04

Watch out

Your bill will be higher: making charges and 3% GST are added at the counter.
