Glossary

G
GST
Tax on your bill: 3% on the gold, 5% on making charges.
H
Hallmark
The BIS stamp that proves purity: logo, carat and fineness, HUID.
HUID
A six-character code unique to each hallmarked piece.
I
IBJA
India Bullion and Jewellers Association. Publishes a daily benchmark rate.
Import duty
Tax on gold brought into India: 15% since May 2026.
K
Karat
Purity out of 24. 22K is 91.6% gold.
M
Making charges
What the shop charges to craft a piece, usually a percentage.
S
Sovereign
Eight grams of gold, also called a pavan.
