Why jewellery is 22K, not 24K

Gold's purity is counted in karats, out of 24. Pure gold, 24K, is so soft that a thin chain would bend out of shape with daily wear.

So jewellers mix in a little copper and silver. 22K is 91.6% gold, which is why its hallmark reads 916.

That is also why the 22K rate is lower than the 24K rate: you pay for the gold that is actually there.
