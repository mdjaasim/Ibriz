Ways to hold gold

The same gold, with different costs and protections.

Jewellery

Extra costMaking charges and GST, not returned
ProtectionBIS hallmark; you store it
SellingAny jeweller, after a deduction

Coins and bars

Extra costA small premium and 3% GST
ProtectionBIS hallmark; you store it
SellingJewellers, after a deduction

Gold ETF

Extra costA small yearly fund fee
ProtectionRegulated by SEBI; nothing to store
SellingOn the exchange, with a demat account

Digital gold

Extra costBuy-sell gap and 3% GST
ProtectionNot regulated by SEBI or RBI
SellingBack through the same app
